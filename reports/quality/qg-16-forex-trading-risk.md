The method is in [docs/design/forex-risk.md](../../docs/design/forex-risk.md) and financial-methods section 21. The tests are rows K-01 to K-06 of the financial test matrix. Every criterion is CRITICAL.

| Criterion | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 16.1 Position sizing is verified against independent reference cases | `tests/forex/test_risk_engine.py`: cases worked by hand (EUR/USD and USD/JPY in a USD account, EUR/GBP through GBP/USD, the same trade in an EUR account, shorts mirroring longs). `test_the_engine_agrees_with_the_oracle`: an independent `Fraction` oracle over five pairs, including JPY, GBP/JPY and USD/KES. `test_the_planned_loss_never_exceeds_the_risk_and_the_size_is_the_largest`: Hypothesis property tests with direct and inverse rates. | 16.2 Pip-value conversions are verified for each quote and account currency case | Same currency, a direct pair, an inverse pair and a cross through USD (`test_conversion_paths`), each kept as an exact fraction. `test_usd_jpy_in_a_usd_account_converts_the_pip_value`, `test_eur_gbp_in_a_usd_account_converts_through_gbp_usd`, `test_the_same_trade_in_another_account_currency_differs`. A missing rate is refused, never assumed (`test_a_missing_rate_is_refused_never_assumed`). Stale and indicative rates are marked. | 16.3 Risk/reward calculations are verified | Before and after costs in the reference cases and `test_costs_reduce_the_size_and_the_reward`. Without a target there is no profit or reward. Copilot figures checked against the tool result (`forex_figures` dataset: risk rows) | Pass |
| 16.4 Margin and leverage calculations are verified | `test_eur_usd_in_a_usd_account` (margin 732.60, leverage 2.20, maximum leverage 30.03, margin use 7.33%), `test_leverage_and_margin_warnings` (leverage over the limit, insufficient margin, a margin-use warning). | 16.5 Costs and slippage are included in outputs | The spread from the latest fresh quote (unknown is warned), slippage and commission from the contract specification, for entry and exit, each shown (`test_costs_reduce_the_size_and_the_reward`, `test_a_usd_quoted_pair_with_costs_from_the_quote_and_the_specification`, `test_a_stale_quote_leaves_the_spread_unknown`). Backtests and paper fills pay the same costs (QG-17.2; `test_paper_api.py`: costs 2 on the stop case). The app shows the costs line (`forex_screens_test.dart`) | Pass |
